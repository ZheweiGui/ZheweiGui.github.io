Dexterous manipulation

DexHOT: Learning Hand–Object Configuration Transfer for Dexterous Manipulation

Zhewei Gui*, Anthony Bolton*, Jiyao Zhang‡, Zeyuan Chen, Qiyang Yan, Tianyu Wang, Hao Dong†

First author

[website][code]

DexHOT formulates dexterous manipulation as hand–object configuration transfer. A single relation-conditioned policy learns from 1.2M feasible configurations across 120 objects and composes long-horizon behaviors through closed-loop subgoals. It achieves 80.5% / 69.4% transfer success on seen / unseen objects in simulation, 61.5% over 200 real-robot transfers, and 90% across six long-horizon tasks.

DexSinGrasp: Learning a Unified Policy for Dexterous Object Singulation and Grasping in Densely Cluttered Environments

Lixin Xu*, Zixuan Liu*, Zhewei Gui, Jingxiang Guo, Zeyu Jiang, Tongzhou Zhang, Zhixuan Xu, Chongkai Gao, Lin Shao

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) · 2025

Contribution: Participated in the RL setting design and completed the sim-to-real system.

[paper][website][code]

DexSinGrasp learns a unified policy for dexterous object singulation and grasping in clutter. Curriculum learning improves generalization across diverse clutter conditions, while policy distillation produces a deployable vision-based strategy. Experiments show higher efficiency and grasping success, particularly in dense clutter.
